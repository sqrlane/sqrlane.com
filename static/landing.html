The gap

Everyone stops one step short.

The desk watches for disruptions and reacts to them by hand. Every tool sold into it owns one half of the job and hands the other half back to a person.

01

Risk platforms

Everstream, Interos, Resilinc. They surface the event and score it. Then it is your problem.

Stops at: the alert

02

Execution tools

The TMS and the visibility stack move the booking once someone has decided it should move. They do not watch the world, and they do not decide.

Starts at: the decision already made

03

SQRlane

Sits on the TMS you already run. Reads the event, triages the book, decides per booking, explains it, drafts the emails, and writes the change back onto the record. Both halves are priced for enterprise; the mid-size desk this is modelled on joins them by hand.

Closes: risk → reroute → comms → the TMS

It watches everything that moves a lane

Not only headlines. Sixty free sources in six families — the wires and the press next to the port, river gauges, weather and sea state, natural hazards, government filings and the rate a reroute is billed at — read together on every run, because a lane is not moved by news alone.

And then it does the TMS work — this is the big one

Every decision comes back as the change it implies on the booking it came from: the exception, the discharge port, the routing code, the revised ETA, each drafted mail on the communication log. Queued against the record, named by the agent that made it, waiting on one click. The desk stops re-typing.

The loop

It opens and closes in the same place.

Risk platforms stop at the alert. Your TMS starts after the decision. Between them sits a person re-keying the consequences, one booking at a time.
