01  What Where the hours actually go

Sixty percent of the day is spent carrying information, not deciding anything.

60%
of the working day goes to coordination — chasing status, searching for information, switching between apps. Only 40% goes to the skilled work.
Asana, Anatomy of Work Index 2022

The five things that eat it

  • Finding it. Which app, whose thread, which version.
  • Retyping it. Into the system, by hand.
  • Deciding what is true. Three places, three versions.
  • Switching. Around 25 times a day, across ten apps.
  • One person holding it. They take leave and the shipment stalls.

Coordination that needs judgement — negotiating a rate, deciding a reroute — is what the forwarder sells. Coordination that only carries information from one place to another is not. We take the second.

The third one is the only one that costs money rather than time. A surcharge agreed in a chat that never reaches the invoice is paid by the forwarder.

The booking is agreed over email. The rate is amended in a chat. The carrier sends an exception notice somewhere else again. None of it reaches the record on its own. A person carries it across, one message at a time.

01  What Who has it, and why it persists

Nobody has fixed it because hiring works.

01

The substitute is headcount, not software

They add people when volume grows, because there is nothing on the market that does the work.

02

There is often nothing to integrate with

Most forwarders have no system of record to connect to. And the channels the work lives in are conversations, not systems — so nobody connected them.

03

So cost scales with volume

Winning a bigger customer means hiring against it. The margin problem gets worse exactly when the business gets better.

That last line is the whole problem. It is not an efficiency story — it is a ceiling on the business, and it is the version a finance director acts on.

01  What The shape of the fix

Read the record, decide against it, and put the answer back on it.

Every hour above is spent carrying something from one place to the record. So the fix is not another place to look. It is the carrying done for you, and the answer landing on the booking it came from — with a person approving it.

That is the How. This section only had to establish that the carrying is the cost.
